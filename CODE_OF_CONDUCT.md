# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Criticise code, not the person who
wrote it.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions — and
whenever someone is representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report problems through
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Sakawat-hossain/PrivateDNS/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting the maintainers directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or ban contributors
whose behaviour they judge inappropriate. Enforcement decisions rest with the
maintainers.
